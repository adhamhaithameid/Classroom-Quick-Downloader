Gridline — Compact Utility

The popup as an instrument panel: everything is a row, every number is tabular, every state has a dot. Settings live in a dense list, the action is a slim filled bar, and motion is 90–200ms with hard stops — built for daily batch users who glance, not gaze.

Why it feels fast
  • Status before action. The strip under the header answers "is anything running?" before you read a single label.
  • Everything is a row. Engine, mode, and flags share one 32px row system with switches and inline mini-segments — no cards, no nesting.
  • Numbers are data. All counts use tabular numerals so columns don't jitter while updating.
  • Progress ticks. The fill advances in discrete per-file steps (steps(6)) — it reads as work completed, not decoration.
  • No travel. Hover is background/border only; nothing lifts, nothing bounces. 90ms in, out before you notice.
Direction-specific decisions
  • Engine rollback copy sits directly under the engine row — one line, always visible, 10px.
  • Flags are switches with live post counts, not pills — the count is the feedback ("12 posts" recalculates as you toggle).
  • "Both" listed as its own row with a combined gradient label treatment (see components.html for the card treatment).
  • History shows speed (2.4 MB/s) and clock times — utility users reconcile against their file manager.

04 · Motion personality
Gridline moves like a cursor, not a wave. Durations: 90ms (hover/switch), 140ms (row state), 200ms (fill ticks). One ease: cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1). No lifts, no glows-on-hover; feedback is instant surface change. The water crest is a single low wave riding per-file ticks. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce: waves hide, fills and counters remain. Full studies in effects.html.
