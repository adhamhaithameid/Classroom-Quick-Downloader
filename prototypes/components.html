C · Download mode — Sequential vs Parallel

A setting row, parallel by default, "3 at a time"

The capability already exists: QUEUE_CONCURRENCY = 3 (entrypoints/background/queue.ts:19, applied at :65) driven by selectNext(snap, cap). Exposing it needs one flat key (downloadConcurrency, 1 = sequential, 3 = parallel — store.ts flat-key constraint) plus this row. Parallel is the default because it is today's behavior and the fastest for multi-file posts.

The row · 360px

Segmented, self-labeling

The values carry their meaning in the labels — "1 at a time" / "3 at a time" — so no jargon like "concurrency" ever reaches the user.

Download mode Parallel is faster for big posts. Switch any time.
1 2 3 4
Specs: parallel pre-selected · switching mid-download is safe (queue re-reads cap at admission) · chips demo shows the real difference: parallel fills 3 at once, sequential fills one-by-one in order.
Behavior contract

What each mode promises

Parallel · 3 at a time (default) — three files download simultaneously; large posts finish roughly 3× sooner; per-file progress still reports individually.

Sequential · 1 at a time — one file at a time, strictly in post order; gentler on weak networks and school machines; total time is longer.

Cancel works identically in both: stops admitting new files, in-flight files finish, partial batch is kept and labeled "Canceled at N / M".

Flat key: downloadConcurrency: 1 | 3 in src/ui/settings/store.ts · read in background at admission time · no migration needed (absent = 3, today's behavior).
D · Classroom-wide action — three candidate labels (task 2.7)

"Download All" is retired — pick a replacement at the Stop Gate

The classroom-level button (v3 only today) must not be called "Download All" — that name is taken by the post-level action and undercounts what this one does. The three candidates below are the same button with different labels; my recommendation is the first. Rename later must be a one-line constant change.

RECOMMENDED

Candidate 1

  • Names the object exactly — everything in this classroom. Shortest label; fits with a count badge at 360px even on 2-line locales.
  • Unambiguous next to the post-level "Download All (N files)" — no shared word, no confusion.
  • Slightly less explicit that it only covers posted materials (not announcements/grades) — the sub-line covers that.
CANDIDATE

Candidate 2

  • Most descriptive of the payload — "materials" is Classroom's own vocabulary.
  • Sets correct expectations: materials only, nothing else.
  • 21 characters; truncates with the badge on narrow locales and still contains "All", echoing the name we are retiring.
CANDIDATE

Candidate 3

  • Energetic and short; reads instantly as "the big one".
  • Zero overlap with the post-level action.
  • Overclaims — users may expect announcements, grades, or other classes. Risky for trust and for support tickets.
