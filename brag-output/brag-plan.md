# Classroom Quick Downloader — One last click

## Planning rubric
1. **Product:** CQD is a browser extension that adds individual and per-post bulk attachment downloads inside Google Classroom.
2. **Strongest verified promise:** One click starts downloads for the attachments in a post. Not a promise of instantaneous transfer or whole-course export.
3. **Visual hook:** A familiar three-attachment post; repeated opening gives way to a single blue Download all pill and green completion feedback.
4. **Actual product material:** Original CQD icon, browser icons, download-control CSS and icon SVGs from the implementation. Enlarged Classroom-style demo with fictional files, identified as an illustrative demo. No real student, instructor, account or course data.
5. **Shortest satisfying film:** 23 seconds, including a five-second installation end card.
6. **Tone:** polished; a tiny tool that solves an annoying problem extremely well. Precise, practical, student-first.
7. **Audio:** original restrained instrumental pulse, 110 BPM, soft click and success accents. No voice: invocation did not request --voice. Original synthesized score avoids the bundled music's undocumented redistribution terms.
8. **Caption:** I built Classroom Quick Downloader to make grabbing class materials less repetitive: download one attachment or a whole post’s files from inside Google Classroom.
9. **Flow:** open a post with attachments → click CQD Download all → downloading and downloaded states. Simulated timing, not a speed benchmark.

## Audience and angle
Students gathering lecture notes, worksheets and slides for study. Lead with the annoying repeated action, demonstrate the solution in its real context, then earn trust through precise language. The product remains the hero; BUILT BY ADHAM is a quiet closing signature.

## Format and identity
Landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 23 seconds. Background #fafcfb; text #1a1a2e; marketing green #1a8b55 / #137a47; native download blue #005DD7 and success green #00A82D. Plus Jakarta Sans, locally bundled; native controls retain their source system font stack. No generic SaaS claims or invented metrics.

## Truthfulness boundaries
Local extension package version is 1.8.0; website response showed 1.5.5. Keep version out of the film. Browser-specific manifests differ, so do not claim Manifest V3 across every browser. No guaranteed hours saved, fastest/best claim, usage counts, testimonials, all-browser guarantee, Google affiliation, whole-classroom download, or universal success claim. Avoid “open source”: license is proprietary/source-available. No “zero telemetry”, “entirely offline”, or “never handles OAuth tokens” claims. Core flow requires the user's existing Classroom access; no new CQD account is needed for that flow.
