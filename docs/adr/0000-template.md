# ADR-0000: Architecture Decision Record Template

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-XXXX
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who was involved

## Context

What forces are in play — technical, operational, organizational. State the problem,
not the solution.

## Decision

The change we're making, stated in active present tense ("We use X for Y").

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what new obligations we take on.
Be honest about the downsides.

## Alternatives Considered

Options we rejected and *why* — this is the part future-us will want most.
