## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our users and infrastructure seriously.

If you discover a security vulnerability within the Extension, Cloudflare Worker, or Oracle Backend, please **DO NOT** open a public issue.

### How to Report

Please email me directly at: **adhamhaithameid@gmail.com**

Include:

* A description of the vulnerability.
* Steps to reproduce.
* Potential impact.

We will acknowledge your email within 48 hours and provide an estimated timeline for a fix.
